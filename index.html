Programs

Explore our programs

Three small classrooms, each shaped around where your child is right now — from the very first weeks through the year before kindergarten.

  • Infant care

    A calm, low-ratio room that follows your baby’s own rhythm for feeding, sleeping and play — not a timetable.

  • Toddler program

    First words, first friendships, and a great deal of pouring, sorting and tidying up again.

  • Montessori preschool

    Mixed-age work cycles long enough that a child can choose a task and see it all the way through.

Our space

First-class spaces
for little learners

Two licensed locations in Gowanus, each set up so a two-year-old can reach everything they need without having to ask for help first.

Indoor play structure

A wooden playhouse, climbing steps and a slide indoors, so gross-motor time never depends on the Brooklyn weather.

Our own kitchen

Breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack, cooked here every morning from organic ingredients.

Nap and quiet room

A separate darkened room for naps, so sleeping children and busy ones never have to share a space.

Montessori work areas

Low open shelves at child height, one of every material, and work cycles long enough that nobody is interrupted halfway through.
