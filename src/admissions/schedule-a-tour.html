On the day

What half an hour gets you

No script and no sales pitch. You will be walked through the rooms by someone who works in them, and asked as many questions as you ask.

  • Walk the classrooms, including the one your child would be in
  • Hear how the program for your child’s age actually runs
  • See the curriculum materials on the shelves, not in a brochure
  • Look at meals — the kitchen, the menu, how allergies are handled
  • See the safety features: check-in, exits, sleep room, cleaning
  • Meet the team members who are free at that moment
  • Talk through your child’s needs — feeding, naps, potty learning, separation, anything that worries you
  • Ask about availability in the room you need
  • Ask tuition questions and what assistance may apply
  • Find out exactly how enrollment works from here
