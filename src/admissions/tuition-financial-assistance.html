Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Tuition

Find answers to common questions about tuition, payments, enrollment, attendance, and more.

01Is there a registration fee?

Yes. There is a one-time, non-refundable registration fee of $50.

02Is a deposit required to enroll?

Yes. One month’s tuition is required to secure your child’s spot. The deposit is non-refundable, but it may be applied toward your child’s final month of tuition when you provide 60 days’ advance written notice.

03Is tuition paid monthly?

Yes. Tuition is billed on the first day of each month, and families have until the 10th to complete payment.

04What happens if payment is late?

Payments not completed by the 10th of the month are subject to a $20 daily late fee.

05Are enrollment hours flexible?

You can choose a standard, extended or maximum day, two to five days a week, as in the tuition rates above. Once enrolled, families follow their contracted hours.

06What if I drop off or pick up outside my contracted hours?

Early drop-offs and late pick-ups outside contracted hours are subject to an additional fee of $1 per minute.

07What age range do you serve?

Children from 6 weeks to 4 years: infant care, toddler care, 2K and free NYC 3-K.

08Are meals and snacks included?

Yes. Breakfast, lunch, and an afternoon snack are included in tuition for every child ready to eat our regular daycare meals.

09Do you accept financial assistance or vouchers?

Yes. We accept ACS and HRA vouchers, All My Children (AMC), and Child Care Aware military child care assistance. Eligibility and authorization vary by program, so please contact us and we will help.

10How do I apply for the NYC 3-K program?

Places go through New York City’s own admissions. Apply through the city, name Cuddle Avenue, and we’ll help you through it. How a 3-K place works.
