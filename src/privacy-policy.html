Who we are

Cuddle Avenue Academy operates child care programs at 69 16th Street, 71 16th Street and 591 3rd Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11215. You can reach us on (800) 620-4221 or at customerservice@cuddleavenue.org.

What this website collects

Only what you type into a form and send us. There are four forms on this site (a tour request, an enrollment inquiry, a tuition inquiry and a general contact form), and between them they ask for:

Your details
Name, email address and, if you give it, a phone number.
Your child’s details
First name, age or date of birth, and the program and start date you are interested in. We ask for the minimum needed to tell you whether we have a place.
Anything you write to us
The message field is free text. Please do not put medical details or any other sensitive information into it. Tell us those on the phone or in person instead.

We use it to answer you. We do not sell it, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not add you to a marketing list because you asked about a tour.

What this website does not do

Worth stating plainly, because it is unusual:

  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no heatmaps, no session recording. We do not know who you are or which pages you read.
  • No cookies. The site sets none. It stores one value in your browser’s session storage (whether you asked for animations to be on or off), which never leaves your device and disappears when you close the tab.
  • No third-party embeds. Fonts, images and the tour videos are all served from this site. Nothing loads from an advertising or tracking network.
  • No tracking links. Links to Google Maps and to our social media accounts are ordinary links; those companies only learn anything if you click.

Who processes what you send

This site is hosted by Netlify, and form submissions are received through Netlify’s form handling before reaching our email. Netlify keeps standard server logs, which include visitors’ IP addresses, as part of running the service. Our email is handled by our email provider.

How long we keep it

Children’s privacy

This website is written for parents and carers, not for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 through it. Where a form asks about a child, we expect an adult to be filling it in.

Your choices

Ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it: email us and we will deal with it. If your child is enrolled, some records must be kept for as long as licensing requires, and we will tell you when that applies.

Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will change the date at the top. Material changes affecting enrolled families will also be told to you directly rather than left on a web page.
