Welcome

Welcome to
Cuddle Avenue Academy

At Cuddle Avenue Academy, we provide nurturing, high-quality daycare and early childhood education for families in Brooklyn, NY. Our programs include Infant/Toddler Care, 2-K, Free NYC 3-K for All Program and Summer camp in warm, thoughtfully designed learning environments.

Our Montessori-inspired approach encourages children to explore, build independence, develop confidence, and discover a love of learning through hands-on experiences. From sensory play, art, music and movement to STEM, language development, outdoor play, and social-emotional learning, each day is designed to support the whole child.

With caring teachers, small-group learning, fresh homemade meals, and extended childcare hours for busy families, Cuddle Avenue Academy is a place where children can feel safe, happy, curious, and truly at home.

Come discover a Brooklyn daycare where little minds grow, friendships begin, and every child is encouraged to shine.

Health & safety

Your child’s safety
comes first

Your child’s safety and well-being are part of everything we do. From secure arrival and pick-up procedures to clean learning spaces and attentive supervision, we create an environment where children can explore, learn, and grow with confidence.

  • Secure check-in & pick-upBrightwheel check-in and verified authorized pick-up procedures.
  • Clean & sanitized spacesClassrooms, toys, and frequently used areas are cleaned and disinfected regularly.
  • Trained & trusted staffOur educators complete required criminal background checks and receive training in health and safety, emergency procedures, CPR, First Aid, and EpiPen administration.
  • Health & allergy awarenessClear illness procedures and careful attention to allergies and individual health needs.

Meals & nutrition

Fresh, homemade,
organic & nut-free

We prepare nutritious, balanced meals with fresh ingredients that children love. Our program is CACFP approved and follows all nutrition and food safety guidelines.

  • Fresh & homemade Made with wholesome ingredients.
  • CACFP approved Nutritious meals that meet federal nutrition standards.
  • Nut-free program A safe environment for all children.

We support each child’s needs

Every child is unique, and we partner with families to make mealtime a positive experience.

Learn more about meals & nutrition

  • Dietary needs

    We support vegetarian, vegan, and other dietary preferences. Families are welcome to provide alternative meals.

  • Infant feeding

    We support breastfeeding and/or formula feeding. Feeding schedules are individualized based on each baby’s needs.

  • Introducing solids

    We do not provide solid foods for infants. Parents are asked to supply all solid foods to avoid any unknown allergies or reactions.

  • Food allergies

    We accommodate food allergies and are happy to accept suitable replacements from parents.
