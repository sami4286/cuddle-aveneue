Welcome

Welcome to
Cuddle Avenue Academy

Cuddle Avenue Academy provides a warm, nurturing early childhood environment where children are encouraged to explore, develop independence, build confidence, and develop a lifelong love of learning.

Our educational approach combines Montessori-inspired principles, developmentally appropriate learning, hands-on experiences, enrichment, and individualized attention.

Why us

Why families choose us

The eight things parents tell us made the decision for them.

  • Licensed child care

    Licensed programs following applicable requirements for health, safety, staffing and supervision.

  • Montessori-inspired learning

    Independence, choice and hands-on exploration, with age-appropriate furniture and materials in every classroom.

  • Small, nurturing environment

    Children are known, supported, cared for and encouraged to grow — by name, every single day.

  • Qualified & carefully screened educators

    Early childhood education and training, plus applicable background checks and clearances.

  • Fresh homemade organic meals

    Breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack, cooked in our own kitchen from organic ingredients.

  • 16+ enrichment experiences

    Yoga, music, art, STEM, Spanish, sign language, cooking, gardening and more, varying by age.

  • Strong family communication

    Daily updates on how your child ate, slept, played and learned — not a summary at pickup.

  • Health & safety-focused environment

    Secure check-in, pediatric CPR and First Aid, emergency preparedness, and daily sanitation.

through the day

Enrichment runs inside the ordinary day, not as an add-on: yoga, music, art, sensory exploration, STEM, puppet shows, cooking, gardening, drama, Spanish, sign language, movement, creative activities, seasonal experiences and special classroom experiences.
