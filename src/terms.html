What this website is

A description of our programs, our practices and how to visit us. It is offered in good faith and kept as accurate as we can, but it is information, not an offer or a contract. Availability, tuition, hours and program details change, and what we tell you directly always takes precedence over what a page says.

Accuracy

Where something on this site is not yet confirmed, we mark it as such rather than quietly guessing. If you find something wrong, please tell us. We would rather fix it than have a family act on it.

Enrollment is separate

Sending a tour request, an enrollment inquiry or a tuition inquiry through this site does not reserve a place, create an obligation on either side, or enroll a child. Enrollment happens through registration paperwork, a deposit and the required documentation. How enrollment works.

Content and images

The text, photographs, films, logo and design of this site belong to Cuddle Avenue Academy or are used with permission. Photographs of children are published only where the family has given permission. Please do not copy, republish or reuse them, particularly the photographs, without asking us first.

Two illustrative photographs in the enrichment section are licensed stock images rather than Cuddle Avenue photography, and are filenamed so as to be obvious. They are not pictures of our children or our rooms.

Links to other sites

We link to Google Maps for directions and to our own social media accounts. Those services have their own terms and privacy practices, and we are not responsible for them.

Acceptable use

Please do not attempt to disrupt the site, submit anything unlawful through its forms, or use them to send bulk or commercial messages. Form submissions reach real people who are also teaching children.
