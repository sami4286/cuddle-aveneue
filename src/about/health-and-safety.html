The people

Who is allowed to be with your child

Every adult who works in a classroom clears the same checks before their first shift, and keeps them current afterwards.

  • Criminal background clearances for every member of staff.
  • Staff health documentation kept on file and up to date.
  • Pediatric CPR and First Aid training.
  • MAT training — Medication Administration Training, for staff who may need to give a prescribed medication.
  • EpiPen and emergency medication training, so an allergic reaction is met by someone who has practised the response.

Beyond the paperwork, the practice that matters most is active supervision: teachers positioned so every child is in sight, and regular headcounts at every transition — room to room, indoors to out, and back again.

The door

Who comes in, and who can leave with your child

Secure check-in and check-out

Arrivals and departures run through Brightwheel QR check-in, so there is a timestamped record of who brought your child and who collected them — not a paper sheet on a clipboard.

Authorized pickup

Only the people you have named may collect your child. Where a face is not known to the teacher on the door, we ask for identity verification before your child goes anywhere. We would rather be awkward for a minute than wrong once.

Every day

Cleaning, sanitation and handwashing

  • Daily cleaning of every classroom, on a written routine rather than when it looks like it needs it.
  • Classroom sanitation of tables, materials and shared surfaces between uses.
  • Handwashing at the points that matter: arrival, before and after eating, after the bathroom, after outdoor play.
  • Diapering sanitation — surfaces cleaned between every change, with handwashing built into the routine.

Food and allergies

Allergy awareness, and no nuts on site

Allergies and dietary needs are agreed with your child’s teacher before the first day, written down, and known to everyone who serves a meal in that room.

No nuts or nut products are permitted on site — ours or anyone else’s, including in packed items brought from home.

We are describing a practice, not a certification: we are not a certified nut-free facility. If your child’s allergy is severe, tell us and we will walk you through exactly how meals and snacks are handled in their room.

The youngest, and the yard

Practices that exist only for some ages

Safe infant sleep

Babies sleep on their backs, in their own crib, on a firm surface with nothing else in it. No exceptions, and no variation because of what happens at home.

Bottle and breast-milk handling

Labelled, stored and warmed to the standards that apply, and logged in Brightwheel so you can see what your baby took and when.

Outdoor safety

Headcounts on the way out and the way back, equipment checked, and an adult positioned where the whole space can be seen.

Weather, sun and water

We watch the forecast and the temperature: sun protection before we go out, hydration while we are out, and days too hot or too cold are spent indoors, where there is room to move.
