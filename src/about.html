Our story

How Cuddle Avenue began

Cuddle Avenue Academy grew out of one educator’s classroom in Brooklyn. Families who had trusted her with their children followed her when she opened her own doors — which is a harder thing to earn than any qualification, and it is still how most families arrive here.

What began as one room is now two licensed buildings a block apart in Gowanus, serving children from six weeks old through preschool. The growth has been deliberate and slow. Small rooms are the point, not a stage to grow out of.

This section is waiting on the school. The founding year, the founder’s name and training, and the reason she started are the most persuasive things this page could carry, and none of them has been supplied. Nothing here is invented — but nothing here is the real story either.

What we are trying to keep

A nurturing environment does not survive growth by accident. It survives because the same people stay, the rooms stay small, and the answer to “can we fit one more in?” is sometimes no. That is the commitment, and it is the one worth judging us on.

Our philosophy

Children learn best when they feel safe

Safe, loved, respected, supported — and from that, confident and curious. That order matters. Everything else we do only works once those first four are true.

Our educational approach combines Montessori-inspired principles, developmentally appropriate learning, hands-on experiences, enrichment, and individualized attention.

What that looks like in a classroom

  • Independence — coats, cubbies, pouring, tidying, done by the child even when it takes longer.
  • Choice — work chosen from a shelf, not handed out.
  • Hands-on learning with real materials rather than worksheets.
  • Practical life as curriculum, not as chores.
  • Exploration, with time long enough to get properly absorbed.
  • Individual development — observed, followed, and never compared out loud.
  • Social-emotional growth treated as a subject in its own right.

Our mission

What we hold ourselves to

  • High-quality early education in every room, at every age.
  • Individualized attention — a child known, not managed.
  • Safe environments, checked and maintained rather than assumed.
  • Independence, practised daily until it is ordinary.
  • Social-emotional development given the same weight as academics.
  • Meaningful learning — work with a purpose the child can see.
  • Family partnerships, because we only have your child for part of the day.

Our educators

Who is in the room

Our educators bring early childhood education, professional classroom experience and child-development training to the rooms they run — and they keep building on it through ongoing study and professional development.

Every one of them clears a criminal background check, holds current health documentation, and is trained in pediatric CPR and First Aid before their first shift. See the full health and safety practice.

Why families choose us

Eight reasons, in detail

The same eight we lead with everywhere else on this site — because if a reason is worth giving, it should survive being read twice.

  • Licensed child care

    We operate licensed programs and follow the applicable requirements for health, safety, staffing, supervision and early childhood care.

  • Montessori-inspired learning

    Independence, confidence, curiosity, responsibility and hands-on exploration, with age-appropriate furniture and materials in every room.

  • Small, nurturing environment

    Children are known, supported, cared for and encouraged — which is only possible while the rooms stay small.

  • Qualified & carefully screened educators

    Background-cleared, health-documented, CPR and First Aid trained, and still studying.

  • Fresh homemade organic meals

    Breakfast, lunch and a snack cooked on site each morning, on one menu for every classroom. See this week’s menu.

  • 16+ enrichment experiences

    Yoga, music, art, STEM, cooking, gardening, drama, Spanish, sign language and more, woven into the ordinary day rather than sold as extras.

  • Strong family communication

    In person at the door, and in Brightwheel through the day. See family partnership below.

  • Health & safety-focused environment

    Secure check-in, trained staff, fire and sprinkler systems, air filtration and daily sanitation. The whole practice.

Family partnership

Where families and teachers grow together

You are your child’s first and most important teacher. We have them for part of a day; you have them for a childhood. Everything below assumes that.

Daily communication

Meals, bottles, naps, diapering, toileting, activities, outdoor time, development, milestones and what happened in the classroom — in person at drop-off and pickup, and in Brightwheel as the day goes.

Getting to know your child

Before the first day we ask about routines, personality, interests, feeding, sleep, comfort strategies, development and your own preferences — and we write it down, because a new teacher should not have to guess.

Settling in

Separation, new teachers, new friends, a new room and a new routine, taken one at a time and at your child’s pace rather than the calendar’s.

Potty learning

Started when your child shows readiness, not at a fixed age. Consistency between home and here, positive encouragement, and constant communication both ways.

Sleep and naps

Individual routines, support through nap transitions, developmentally appropriate rest, and infant safe sleep practised without exception.

Feeding partnership

Breast milk, formula, bottles, feeding schedules, starting solids, allergies and dietary needs — agreed with you and revisited whenever things change.

Developmental milestones

Language, motor development, social-emotional growth, independence, self-help skills and early academics, shared as they happen rather than saved for a report.

Family corner & community

Family photos in the classroom, and the cultures, languages, traditions and celebrations children bring with them treated as something the whole room learns from.
