A typical day

A Day in Our Infant Classroom

Every baby has their own rhythm, and we respect that. While we have a daily schedule filled with learning, play, and enrichment activities, feeding and nap times are always adjusted to each baby’s individual needs. Our goal is to provide a balance of loving care, exploration, and early learning throughout the day.

Follows your baby’s own feeding and sleep schedule

This is an example of our daily routine. Infants follow their individual feeding and sleeping schedules, and activities are adjusted based on their age, development, and needs. Some babies may nap two or three times throughout the day.

Enrichments

More Ways to Explore, Grow and Thrive

Our enrichment activities are specially designed for infants to spark curiosity, support development and build confidence, all through fun, age-appropriate experiences.

  • Baby Sign Language

    Helps babies express their needs, build communication skills and strengthen the parent–teacher connection.

  • Music Class

    Songs, rhythm and instruments introduce sounds, support language development and make learning fun.

  • Baby Yoga

    Gentle movement and stretching support motor skills, body awareness and a sense of calm.

  • Spanish Class

    Playful exposure to a second language builds listening skills, memory and cultural awareness.

  • Sensory Development

    Exploring different textures, colors and materials strengthens the senses, curiosity and brain development.

  • STEM & Confidence Building

    Simple, hands-on exploration introduces early problem-solving skills and encourages independence and confidence.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Infant Care

01What ages do you accept in your infant program?

Our infant program welcomes children from 6 weeks to 12 months. We provide individualized care and age-appropriate learning experiences that support each child’s development.

02Do infants follow a daily schedule?

Yes. Our infant classroom has a daily routine that includes enrichment activities, sensory exploration, movement, storytime, and early learning experiences. Feeding and sleeping schedules are individualized to each child’s needs.

03How do you handle feeding schedules?

We follow each child’s individual feeding schedule, including the bottle-feeding routine provided by parents. Our teachers work closely with families to keep feeding consistent between home and daycare.

04Do you provide meals for infants?

Yes. Breakfast, lunch, and an afternoon snack are included in tuition for infants who are developmentally ready to eat our regular daycare meals. Parents must provide purées or specially prepared infant foods.

05What if my child needs more than two naps?

Younger infants often need two or three naps a day. Our teachers follow each child’s individual sleep schedule.

06What learning and enrichment do infants take part in?

Our infant program includes sensory exploration, storytime, fine motor activities and early communication, plus enrichment classes: baby sign language, live music and guitar, baby yoga, Spanish, sensory development, and confidence-building activities. All enrichment classes and educational activities are included in tuition.

07How will I receive updates during the day?

We use the Brightwheel app to share feeding updates, diaper changes, naps, photos, videos, classroom activities, and important announcements. Parents can message their child’s teacher directly through Brightwheel.

08How do you keep infants safe?

Our infant classroom is supervised by trained childcare professionals who follow applicable health and safety regulations. Our teachers complete required background checks and safety training, including CPR and First Aid. We also maintain regular cleaning and sanitizing procedures.

09What happens if my child becomes sick?

If your child becomes sick while at daycare, we contact you and follow our illness policy. Children with a fever must remain home until they have been fever-free for at least 24 hours without fever-reducing medication. A doctor’s note is also required before returning.

10Are vaccinations required?

Yes. Children must meet applicable New York State childcare immunization requirements. Families must provide updated medical and vaccination records.

11Is tuition charged monthly?

Yes. Tuition is due on the 1st of each month, with payment required by the 10th to avoid late fees.

12Can I schedule a tour before enrolling?

Absolutely! We encourage families to visit our infant classroom, meet our teachers, and learn more about our daily routines and educational approach. You can schedule an in-person tour through our website.
