Our 3-K program

Ready to Learn, Ready for Kindergarten

Our free 3-K classroom helps children separate confidently at the door, ask an adult for help, sit down to something difficult, share a table, and recover from a disappointment. Academics follow, and a child with these skills arrives at kindergarten ready to learn.

Our approach

What a 3‑K Year Covers

We plan and observe with Teaching Strategies GOLD, the framework the city expects, so what a teacher notices becomes the next thing your child is offered. Sensory and outdoor learning happen most days, in most weather.

  1. Early Literacy

    Sounds, letters, stories and first writing

  2. Mathematics

    Counting, pattern, shape and measurement

  3. Language

    Conversation and vocabulary

  4. Social-Emotional

    Friendship and naming feelings

  5. Independence

    Dressing, serving and caring for the room

Enrichments

Beyond the Classroom: 3‑K Enrichment

Our enrichment program introduces children to music, movement, language, creativity, and hands-on learning through fun, age-appropriate experiences. Enrichment happens inside the ordinary day, never as a paid extra.

  • Sign Language

    Learning simple signs to support communication and language development.

  • Live Music & Guitar

    A visiting music teacher joins us weekly for interactive songs, rhythm, movement, and musical activities.

  • Spanish Language

    A visiting Spanish teacher introduces children to Spanish through songs, games, and interactive learning once a week.

  • Yoga

    Playful stretching and movement activities that support balance, coordination, and body awareness.

  • Arts & Crafts

    Creative projects that encourage imagination, self-expression, and fine motor development.

  • Sensory Development

    Exploring textures, colors, sounds, and different materials through hands-on activities.

  • STEAM

    Simple science, technology, engineering, art, and math activities that encourage curiosity and problem-solving.

  • Gardening

    Age-appropriate gardening experiences that introduce children to plants, nature, and caring for living things.

  • Little Chefs: Cooking Class

    Simple, supervised cooking activities that introduce children to ingredients, food preparation, and practical life skills.

  • Drama & Pretend Play

    Imaginative activities, storytelling, puppets, and role-playing that support creativity and social development.

Enrolling

How a 3‑K Place Works

Places go through New York City’s own admissions. Apply through the city, name us, and we’ll help you through it.

  1. 1

    Step 1

    Come and See the Room

    Visit on a weekday morning to see the classroom and meet the teacher.

  2. 2

    Step 2

    Apply Through the City

    It’s the city’s application. List programs in order of preference. Cuddle Avenue is one you can name.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    We Help You Through It

    Call us while you apply. We’ll tell you where you are in the cycle and the current deadlines.

  4. 4

    Step 4

    Add Extended Care

    Arranged directly with us, separately from the city’s offer, only for the hours you need.
