Our summer camp

A Summer Made for Small Children

The same familiar rooms, with longer outdoors, more water play, more making and a new theme every week. The teachers and the kitchen stay the same, so summer days still feel familiar while every week brings a whole new set of things to try.

Weekly themes

A New Theme Every Week

Each week has one big theme. The art, stories, cooking and outdoor games all follow it, and the week ends with a dress-up theme day.

  1. Art

    Made for the week’s theme

  2. Songs & Stories

    Chosen to fit it

  3. Cooking

    A recipe that goes with it

  4. Outdoor Play

    Games and water play built around it

  5. Theme Day

    Everyone dresses up to end the week

Outdoors

Water and Outdoor Play

Water tables, sprinklers and the sort of soaking that needs a full change of clothes. Sunscreen goes on before we go out, water is offered constantly while we are out, and the shade is used properly.

  • Splash and Water Play

    Pack a towel, a swim nappy where relevant, and shoes that survive being wet

  • Sun and Hydration

    Sunscreen, shade and plenty of water all day

  • Very Hot Days

    We play indoors, with room to move

Activities

What Camp Is Made Of

Six kinds of play, looser than a school term and outdoors whenever the weather allows.

  • Art

    Bigger, messier projects, and mostly outdoors.

  • Sensory Play

    Sand, water, ice and dough to squeeze, pour and explore.

  • STEM

    Building, floating, melting and growing.

  • Cooking

    Simple summer food the children make and eat.

  • Gardening

    The part of the year when things really grow.

  • Music & Movement

    Songs, rhythm and dancing, outdoors whenever we can.
