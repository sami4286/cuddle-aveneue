Enrichments

Beyond the Classroom: Toddler Enrichment

Our toddler enrichment program introduces children to music, movement, language, creativity, and hands-on learning. All enrichment classes are included in tuition, and activities may change with the seasons, holidays, and curriculum themes.

  • Baby Sign Language

    Introduces simple signs to help toddlers communicate their needs, feelings, and everyday experiences.

  • Live Music & Guitar

    A visiting music teacher joins our classroom weekly for interactive songs, rhythm, movement, and musical exploration.

  • Spanish Language

    A visiting Spanish teacher introduces toddlers to Spanish through songs, interactive games, repetition, and age-appropriate activities.

  • Toddler Yoga

    Gentle movements, stretching, and playful poses help children develop balance, coordination, body awareness, and concentration.

  • Arts & Crafts

    Age-appropriate creative projects encourage imagination, self-expression, fine motor development, and exploration of different materials.

  • Sensory Development

    Hands-on activities encourage children to explore textures, sounds, colors, shapes, and materials while developing sensory awareness.

  • STEAM Exploration

    Simple science, technology, engineering, art, and math experiences introduce early problem-solving, discovery, and creative thinking.

  • Drama & Pretend Play

    Imaginative activities, puppets, storytelling, and role-playing help children develop creativity, communication, and social skills.

  • Social-Emotional Learning

    Positive interactions and age-appropriate activities help toddlers develop confidence, recognize emotions, practice kindness, and build relationships.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Toddler Care

Choosing the right daycare is an important decision. Here are answers to the questions families ask most about our toddler program.

01What ages do you accept in your toddler program?

Our toddler program welcomes children ages 12–24 months. We provide a safe, nurturing, and engaging environment that supports early learning, independence, and social development.

02Is tuition charged monthly?

Yes. Tuition is billed monthly on the first day of each month. Payments must be received by the 10th to avoid late payment fees.

03Are drop-off and pick-up times flexible?

Families are required to follow their contracted hours. Early drop-offs and late pick-ups outside contracted hours are subject to an additional fee of $1 per minute.

04What happens if my child is sick?

If your child becomes sick at daycare, we contact you and follow our illness policy. Children with a fever must remain home until they have been fever-free for at least 24 hours without fever-reducing medication. A doctor’s note is also required before returning.

05Will parents be notified if there is an illness in the classroom?

Yes. If we become aware of a contagious illness or notice similar symptoms among children, we notify families so they can monitor their children and take appropriate precautions.

06How do parents receive daily updates?

We use the Brightwheel app to share photos, videos, daily reports, meals, learning activities, and important classroom updates. Brightwheel also gives you curriculum information, calendars, billing, and direct messaging with our teachers, so you can message your child’s teacher whenever you have a question.

07What safety and cleaning procedures do you follow?

Our classrooms, toys, and frequently touched surfaces are cleaned and sanitized regularly. Classroom carpets are cleaned weekly on Fridays, and we use air purifiers and HVAC ventilation systems to help maintain indoor air quality.

08Are your teachers trained and background checked?

Yes. All teachers complete required criminal background checks and receive CPR, First Aid, health and safety, emergency procedure, and EpiPen training. Our staff also complete ongoing early childhood education training, including 15 hours of professional development annually, and we require relevant childcare experience and appropriate staff vaccinations.

09Are vaccinations required?

Yes. Children must meet applicable New York State childcare immunization requirements, including the annual influenza vaccine requirement. We monitor vaccination records and notify parents when updated documentation is needed.

10What if my child does not want to nap?

Children who do not fall asleep are encouraged to rest quietly on their cots during nap time. Our teachers remain present and supervise children throughout the rest period.

11Are meals and enrichment classes included in tuition?

Yes. Breakfast, lunch, and an afternoon snack are included, along with all enrichment classes: music, Spanish, yoga, sign language, sensory development, STEAM, and other age-appropriate activities. Parents are also welcome to provide alternative meals.

12Do you help with potty training?

Yes. We work together with parents when they are ready to begin potty training, and follow a consistent approach to support each child’s individual progress.

13What curriculum do you follow?

We follow the Experience Curriculum, which focuses on hands-on learning, early childhood development, and age-appropriate educational experiences. Parents can follow daily learning activities and projects in Brightwheel.

14Can I schedule a tour before enrolling?

Absolutely! We encourage families to visit our toddler classroom, meet our teachers, and learn more about our daily routines and educational approach. You can schedule an in-person tour through our website.
