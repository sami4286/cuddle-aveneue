Our 2K program

A Safe and Nurturing Place to Explore and Grow

Our 2K program is designed for children ages 2 to 3, providing a loving and supportive environment where they build independence, develop new skills, and form meaningful friendships through play and discovery.

Our approach

Experience Curriculum

We follow the Experience Curriculum, a research-based approach that supports each child’s development through play, discovery, and real-life experiences. Our 2K program focuses on building essential skills across all areas in a way that feels natural and meaningful.

  1. Cognitive

    Problem solving and curiosity

  2. Physical

    Gross and fine motor skills

  3. Social & Emotional

    Friendship, empathy and confidence

  4. Language

    Communication and expression

  5. Creative

    Imagination and self-expression

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Our 2K Program

01What ages do you accept in your 2K program?

Our 2K program welcomes children ages 2–3 years old. We provide age-appropriate learning experiences that encourage independence, confidence, communication, and social development.

02Do children go outside every day?

Yes. Weather permitting, children enjoy outdoor play every day, typically for about 30–60 minutes depending on the weather, daily activities, and classroom schedule.

03What curriculum do you follow?

We follow the Experience Curriculum, which introduces children to early learning concepts through hands-on activities, stories, music, creative projects, and interactive experiences.

04Do you support potty training?

Yes. We work closely with families when they are ready to begin potty training, and our teachers follow a consistent approach to support each child’s individual progress.

05Are meals included, and can you accommodate dietary needs?

Yes. Breakfast, lunch, and an afternoon snack are included in tuition. We accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and other dietary preferences and food allergies, and parents are welcome to provide alternative meals.

06Are enrichment classes included in tuition?

Yes. All enrichment classes, including music, Spanish, yoga, sign language, STEAM, gardening, cooking, and other age-appropriate activities, are included in tuition.

07Is tuition charged monthly?

Yes. Tuition is billed on the first day of each month, and families have until the 10th to complete payment. Late payments are subject to a $20 daily late fee.

08Are drop-off and pick-up times flexible?

Families are required to follow their contracted hours. Early drop-offs and late pick-ups outside contracted hours are subject to an additional fee of $1 per minute.

09What if my child does not want to nap?

Children who do not fall asleep are encouraged to rest quietly on their cots during nap time. Our teachers remain present and supervise children throughout the rest period.

10How do parents receive daily updates?

We use the Brightwheel app to share photos, videos, meals, daily activities, curriculum updates, and important announcements. Parents can also message teachers directly through the app.

11What happens if my child is sick?

If your child becomes sick at daycare, we contact you and follow our illness policy. Children with a fever must remain home until they have been fever-free for at least 24 hours without fever-reducing medication. A doctor’s note is also required before returning.

12Are vaccinations required?

Yes. Children must meet applicable New York State childcare immunization requirements, including the annual influenza vaccine requirement. Families must provide updated medical and vaccination records.

13Are your teachers trained and background checked?

Yes. Our teachers complete required background checks and receive CPR, First Aid, health and safety, and emergency procedure training, and take part in ongoing early childhood education training.

14Can I schedule a tour before enrolling?

Absolutely! We encourage families to visit our 2K classroom, meet our teachers, and learn more about our program. You can schedule an in-person tour through our website.
