Why families choose Cuddle Avenue

Because choosing childcare is about more than finding a place. It’s about finding the right place for your child.

  • Individualized care Every child is known, supported, and encouraged at their own pace.
  • Experienced & caring educators A nurturing team focused on your child’s safety, happiness, and development.
  • Montessori-inspired learning Hands-on experiences build independence, confidence, curiosity, and a love of learning.
  • Stay connected throughout the day Through Brightwheel, families receive frequent photos, videos, and updates so you can share in your child’s day, even when you’re apart.
  • Fresh, homemade meals Nutritious, thoughtfully prepared meals are served fresh to support growing little bodies.
