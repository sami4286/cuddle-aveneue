How it works

From first look to first day

  • Step 1 Explore the programs Find the room that matches your child’s age — all five are here, with ages, hours and what a day holds.
  • Step 2 Schedule a tour About thirty minutes on a weekday morning, while the classrooms are running. Book a time.
  • Step 3 Discuss availability We tell you honestly what we have in that room and when — including when the honest answer is a waitlist.
  • Step 4 Complete registration The registration paperwork and the deposit that holds your child’s place.
  • Step 5 Submit the required documentation Health forms, immunisation records and the authorized pickup list. We tell you exactly what is needed and by when.
  • Step 6 Prepare for the first day What to pack, how settling in will go, and how the first week is paced so it is gentle on everyone.

Worth knowing first

NYC 3-K for All is free

If your child turns three in the calendar year, the school day is free, funded by New York City — 8:40am to 3:00pm, five days a week, in our own classroom with our own teachers.

Before and after care extends that to 7:30am–6:00pm for an additional out-of-pocket fee, so a free school day and a working day can be the same thing.
