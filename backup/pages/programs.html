Our programs

Choose by your child’s age

From your baby’s first months to the year before Pre-K, plus a summer program. Each card has the essentials; Explore opens the full picture of that program.

  • Infant care

    Birth – 12 months

    Nurturing infant care with individualized feeding, sleep, sensory play, and loving attention during your baby’s earliest months.

    Your baby keeps their own feeding and sleep routine instead of fitting into a room schedule. Breastfeeding and bottle feeding are fully supported, and you hear how the day is going through Brightwheel.

    • Individual feeding and nap schedules
    • Tummy time, language and sensory play
    • Daily photos and updates in Brightwheel

    591 3rd Avenue
    Monday – Friday, 7:30am – 6:00pm

    Explore infant care →

  • Toddler care

    12 – 24 months

    A safe, engaging toddler program filled with hands-on learning, movement, language development, and opportunities to build independence.

    This is the year of “me do it”: pouring, carrying, climbing and dressing themselves, with first words and first friendships along the way. Potty learning starts when your child is ready, not before.

    • Language and social-emotional growth
    • Practical life and real independence
    • Music, yoga, Spanish and sign language

    591 3rd Avenue · 69 16th Street
    Monday – Friday, 7:30am – 6:00pm

    Explore toddler care →

  • 2K program

    2 – 3 years

    A Montessori-inspired preschool experience where children learn through play, build confidence, develop social skills, and prepare for 3-K.

    Two-year-olds get a real Montessori work cycle: they choose their work and are given the time to concentrate on it. Early literacy and early mathematics begin here, alongside practical life and potty support.

    • Early literacy and early mathematics
    • A daily Montessori work cycle
    • A smooth step into our free 3-K

    69 16th Street
    Monday – Friday, 7:30am – 6:00pm

    Explore 2K →

  • NYC 3-K for All

    3 – 4 years

    Free NYC 3-K combining purposeful play, early academics, creativity, and social-emotional learning to build a strong foundation for Pre-K.

    The school day, 8:40am to 3:00pm, is free for any child turning three in the calendar year, with breakfast, lunch and snack included. Before and after care extend the day to 7:30am–6:00pm for a fee.

    • A free full school day
    • Breakfast, lunch and snack included
    • Optional before and after care

    69 16th Street
    Free day 8:40am – 3:00pm · extended care 7:30am – 6:00pm

    Explore NYC 3-K →

  • Summer camp

    2 – 5 years

    A fun-filled summer program with art, STEM, sensory experiences, outdoor play, water activities, music, and exciting weekly themes.

    Summer camp runs in our own classrooms with our own staff and kitchen, so children spend the summer somewhere familiar while the days fill with splash play, theme days and special events. Hours and dates change each year, so call us for this summer’s details.

    • Water play and plenty of outdoor time
    • Weekly themes and special events
    • Art, cooking, gardening and STEM
