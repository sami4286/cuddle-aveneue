The basics

What ages do you accept?

6 weeks to five years: infant care (6 weeks to 12 months), toddler care (12 to 24 months), 2K (2 to 3 years), NYC 3-K for All (3 to 4 years) and summer camp (2 to 5 years).

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 6:00pm, in every program. The free NYC 3-K school day runs 8:40am to 3:00pm, with optional before and after care.

Where are your locations?

591 3rd Avenue (Infant & Toddler Program), 69 16th Street (NYC 3-K for All) and 71 16th Street (2K Program), all in Park Slope, Brooklyn 11215, within a block of each other. Addresses and directions

Programs

Do you offer infant care?

Yes, from 6 weeks to 12 months, on each child’s own feeding and sleep schedule. Learn more

Do you offer toddler care?

Yes, for children 12 to 24 months, with hands-on learning and potty-learning support. Learn more

Do you offer 2K?

Yes, a Montessori-inspired year for children 2 to 3 years old that prepares them for 3-K. Learn more

Is NYC 3-K free?

Yes. The school day, 8:40am to 3:00pm, is funded by New York City, meals included. Only before and after care is paid. Learn more

Is before and after care available?

Yes, for an additional fee: 7:30 to 8:40am and 3:00 to 6:00pm. Ask about rates

Do you run a summer camp?

Yes, for ages 2 to 5, with outdoor and water play, art, STEM, cooking and weekly themes. Learn more

Meals

Do you provide meals?

Yes. Breakfast, lunch and snack are cooked fresh in our own kitchen and included in tuition. See the menu

Are you nut-free?

No nuts or nut products are allowed on site. We are not a certified nut-free facility, so please tell us about any severe allergy. Learn more

What about allergies and special diets?

They are agreed with your child’s teacher before the first day and known to everyone who serves your child. Learn more

Do you support breastfeeding?

Yes. Nursing parents are welcome to feed here, and we take labelled breast milk and formula. Learn more

Daily care

How are naps handled?

Individually. Infants sleep on their own schedule, on their backs in their own crib. Older children rest after lunch on their own mat.

Do you help with potty learning?

Yes, starting when your child shows readiness. Children starting 3-K must be fully potty trained, and our 2K year helps them get there. Learn more

Do children go outside?

Most days, with headcounts on the way out and back, sun protection and water breaks. Outdoor safety

Learning

What curriculum do you use?

A Montessori-inspired approach with hands-on learning. In NYC 3-K we also use Teaching Strategies GOLD.

What enrichment do you offer?

Yoga, music, art, sensory play, STEM, puppet shows, cooking, gardening, drama, Spanish and sign language, all included.

Health & safety

Are staff background checked and trained?

Yes. Every staff member has criminal background clearances, health documentation and pediatric CPR and First Aid training, plus MAT and EpiPen training. Learn more

Who can pick up my child?

Only people you authorize. When someone new is picking up, let us know in advance with their full name. They must be 18 or older and show photo ID. Pickup policy

How do you keep families updated?

In person at drop-off and pickup, and through Brightwheel during the day, with meals, naps, activities and photos. About Brightwheel

Tuition

How much is tuition?

Monthly tuition depends on the schedule and days a week. The NYC 3-K school day is free. See tuition

Do you accept HRA, ACS or military assistance?

Yes, all three. Each agency decides who is eligible, and we help with the paperwork on our side. Learn more
