Allergies & individual diets

Agreed before
the first day

Allergies, cultural and religious needs and medical diets are written down with your child’s teacher and known to everyone who serves your child.

How we handle allergies
  • No nuts on siteFrom anyone, including food brought from home.
  • Written downAgreed with you before your child starts.
  • Updated the same dayIf something changes, tell us and it changes here.
  • EpiPen trainedStaff trained in EpiPen and emergency medication.

Severe allergy? We are not a certified nut-free facility, so please talk to us first.

Breastfeeding & bottles

Support for however you feed your baby

Nursing parents are welcome to feed here at drop-off, lunch or pickup, in a quiet space, with no need to arrange it ahead.

Bottles, formula and schedules

  • Breast milk

    Bring it labelled with your baby’s name and the date. We refrigerate it, use the oldest first and warm it gently.

  • Formula

    Bring your own, labelled, and tell us how you prepare it. We make it up the same way.

  • Feeding schedule

    Your baby’s own schedule, agreed with you on the first day and adjusted as they grow.

  • Tracked in Brightwheel

    Every bottle and feed is logged as it happens, so you can see what your baby took and when.

Starting solids

Solids come from home

Parents provide all solid foods for infants, so every new food is tried at home first. Tell us how to serve it and we will follow it exactly.
