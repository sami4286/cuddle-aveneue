Staff safety

Trained & qualified staff

Every adult who works in a classroom clears the same checks before their first shift, and keeps them current afterwards. Beyond the paperwork, the practice that matters most is active supervision: teachers positioned so every child is in sight, and regular headcounts at every transition.

  1. 1

    Criminal background clearances

    For every member of staff, before their first shift.

  2. 2

    Staff health documentation

    Kept on file and kept up to date.

  3. 3

    Pediatric CPR and First Aid

    Training held current by everyone in a classroom.

  4. 4

    MAT training

    Medication Administration Training, for staff who may need to give a prescribed medication.

  5. 5

    EpiPen and emergency medication

    So an allergic reaction is met by someone who has practised the response.

Health & illness

When a child is unwell

If your child becomes sick at daycare, we contact you and follow our illness policy, and we keep every classroom clean.

  • Fever-free for 24 hours

    A child with a fever stays home until fever-free for at least 24 hours without fever-reducing medication.

  • Doctor’s note

    A doctor’s note is required before returning to daycare after a fever.

  • We keep you informed

    We call you if your child becomes unwell, and tell families when a contagious illness appears in the classroom.

  • Clean classrooms

    Toys and surfaces are sanitized regularly, carpets are cleaned every Friday, and air purifiers run in every room.

Vaccinations. Children must meet New York State childcare immunization requirements, including the annual flu vaccine, and families provide updated medical and vaccination records.
